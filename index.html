About

I am an Assistant Professor at TU Wien Informatics where I lead the learnCPS lab. We bring together learning, optimization, and control theory to study and develop intelligent and interacting Cyber-Physical-Human Systems.

I received my Diploma in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the National Technical University of Athens, Greece, where I worked on neuro-robotics and human-robot interaction with Prof. Kostas J. Kyriakopoulos. I then pursued a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering at the University of Maryland, College Park, working with Prof. John S. Baras on stochastic optimization, and inference and control of cyber-physical systems. After that, I joined KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Stockholm, as a postdoctoral associate, where I worked with Prof. Karl Henrik Johansson on learning-based control, explainable AI, and communication-aware robotics, collaborating with Ericsson Research as the PI of a 2-year SSF project. In 2026, I joined TU Wien Informatics as an Assistant Professor.


Research Interests

We tackle inference and control problems in various Cyber-Physical-Human Systems, from autonomous cars to UAV swarms, and from industrial robots to collaborative human-robot systems. We focus on the analysis of the underlying learning algorithms using principles from:

  • hybrid systems theory
  • stochastic optimization
  • robust and risk-sensitive control
  • knowledge representation and reasoning
  • group-invariant and hierarchical topologies
  • homotopy optimization and bifurcation theory

Beyond fundamental research on Optimization, Learning Theory, (Hybrid) Systems and Control Theory, and Multi-Agent Systems, we are interested in a wide range of applications, including:

  • Robotics, Autonomous Vehicles, UAVs
  • Explainable AI & Learning-based Control
  • Human-Robot Interaction & Collaboration
  • Risk-Sensitive Reinforcement Learning
  • Communication-aware Robotics
  • Swarm Dynamics Identification
  • Intelligent Transportation
  • Smart Cities
